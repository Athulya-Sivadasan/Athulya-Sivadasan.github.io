Flagship · NeST Digital Internship

Predicta — Edge-AI Predictive Maintenance

An on-device predictive maintenance system for rotating machinery. An ESP32 fuses vibration (MPU6050), temperature (DS18B20), and current (ACS712) readings, then runs an LSTM via TensorFlow Lite for Microcontrollers to classify machine health across five states — Normal, Watch, Warning, Critical, Failure — streamed live to a Progressive Web App dashboard.
