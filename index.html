Focus Areas

Where I concentrate my engineering effort

Four disciplines I return to consistently — each a distinct layer of the same underlying stack, from silicon to sensor to inference. Select any discipline to expand its scope.

Deploying compact neural architectures — including LSTM-based sequence models — onto resource-constrained microcontrollers, enabling low-latency inference without dependence on cloud infrastructure. This work emphasizes model quantization, memory-conscious architecture selection, and deterministic real-time classification suitable for embedded deployment.
Engineering firmware and sensor-interfacing pipelines that bridge physical instrumentation with connected dashboards, using ESP32 and Arduino-based architectures to capture, process, and transmit real-world telemetry. This includes multi-sensor fusion — vibration, thermal, and current sensing — alongside asynchronous data pipelines and lightweight communication protocols suited for constrained environments.
Formulating and verifying digital logic architectures in Verilog HDL — from combinational and sequential building blocks to fully self-checking testbenches validated within Xilinx Vivado. This practice emphasizes rigorous functional verification and design-for-test principles, ensuring correctness ahead of physical implementation.
Investigating the intersection of sensing, actuation, and embedded control — extending intelligent systems beyond passive monitoring into responsive, physically interactive behavior. Ongoing exploration includes motor control, feedback loops, and the integration of embedded intelligence with mechanical actuation.
About

Engineering at the sensor-to-inference boundary

I am a final-year B.Tech candidate in Electronics & Communication Engineering at Ahalia School of Engineering and Technology, affiliated with the APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University. My engineering interests are situated at the confluence of hardware and computational intelligence — specifically, sensor instrumentation, embedded firmware architecture, and the deployment of machine learning inference at the edge.

My project portfolio spans predictive maintenance systems, digital-twin health monitoring, and VLSI logic design, each documented comprehensively from schematic conception through deployed implementation. Beyond the laboratory, I engage in competitive public speaking and pursue portrait, documentary, and wildlife photography across the Kerala landscape.
